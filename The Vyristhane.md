The Vyristhane cuts an imposing, stately figure. A tall, straight-backed man in a dark robe, with a many-spiked, dark crown and a sword with a blade of gleaming glass. He governs the realm of the distinction between self and other. He appears only in mirrors, surrounded by a thick black mist, occasionally observing those who use them. Despite his unsettling appearance, a mirror-encounter with him is considered a mark of good fortune, and is quite uncommon. He is also known as "The King of the Space Between." Sometimes, people will encounter him outside of a mirror. Always when they are alone, always when they are traveling. He will appear to them as a person who reflects their own default reaction and assumptions of The Stranger back to them. For example, someone who is inclined to view strangers with suspicion may encounter him as a shifty, untrustworthy type, maybe even as an open brigand who demands payment. Whereas those who assume the best of strangers by default may encounter a traveling companion who stays with them for a time. A new friend with whom they can carry on a long and profound conversation that challenges them on some of their deepest assumptions, before parting ways.

Since the death of [[The AllFather]], he is becoming gradually more trapped within mirrors and unable to exert influence outside of them.

His name comes from old English roots: vyris comes from a word meaning an individual's worth or value, and by extension their *values*, while the thane part comes from an Old English word denoting a boundary stone or marker, while also carrying hints of the "Thane" title, meaning something like "local ruler". So The Vyristhane is the boundary-stone between competing realms of values, and thereby, by synecdoche, between the people who hold them.
