This illness has only been observed after [[The death of the AllFather]]. Some believe it to be a curse brought about by that event, while others think it some bane brough by [[Yggdrasil]], and yet others consider it a key to the afterlife. Whatever its true nature, it is incredibly painful, and untreatable. It progresses in three stages. First, those who have contracted it have dreams of flying. This stage may last a week to a month. Second, the invalid experiences a massively increased metabolism, as their bones begin to hollow. This stage typically lasts no more than a day or two. Lastly, feathered wings explode from out of their back in the final stage, the moment of death. Feathers from their wings are potent alchemical reagents [TODO: Potent how? For what?]. The bones of those who have died from the illness seem to contain light in their hollows, as can be observed if you break one of them in a dark room.
