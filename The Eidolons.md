The Eidolons are fragments of the logos as embodied by [[The AllFather]] that were scattered in the moment of his death. Each Eidolon is one of the fundamental rules that structure and orient reality. Likewise, each Eidolon manifests and influences the world via a folklore-ic being.
1. Eidolon of Entropy:
	1. Represents the arrow of time
	2. Embodied in TODO
2. Eidolon of Alethia:
	1. Represents the distinction between truth and falsehood
	2. Embodied in [[The Spindlewolf]]
3. Eidolon of Cynosure
	1. Represents location and place
	2. Embodied in [[Ourokthon]]
4. Eidolon of Nisus
	1. Represents will and capacity to accomplish
	2. Embodied in TODO
5. Eidolon of Prognos
	1. Represents wisdom and foresight
	2. Embodied in [[The Incunabulist]] of [[Tavhra's Trod]]
6. Eidolon of Exlineation
	1. Represents the distinction between self and other
	2. Embodied in [[The Vyristhane]]
