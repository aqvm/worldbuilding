As [[The AllFather]]'s steeped in the bottom of [[The Well of Mimir]], an exploratory root from [[Yggdrasil]] wound through the rocks at the base of the well. Prolonged contact with the eye in the water blessed this root with its own consciousness, and it resolved to stand against its progenitor. It now wanders the world as the armarius of [[Tavhra's Trod]], seeking to learn or discover how to stave off the metaphysical (and physical) predations of [[Yggdrasil]]. He hides his ultimately wooden nature beneath voluminous robes and a deep hood, and is known for answering questions with riddles.
