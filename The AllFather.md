A benevolent cosmic entity, the AllFather is the creator-god of [[world-name]]. Possessed of all the faculties necessary for creation, he lacked only the substance. He exchanged [[The Eidolon of Prognos|his right eye]] for [[Yggdrasil]]'s raw material, leaving it in [[The Well of Mimir]] at the base of [[Yggdrasil]]. He is the logos of [[world-name]], and embodies [[The Eidolons|the foundational principles that make it intelligible]] to those who live within it. Upon his death, his [[The Eidolons]] were scattered throughout the world.
