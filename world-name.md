This world was created by a [[deity]] known as [[The AllFather]]. He was a benevolent cosmic being possessed of great sagacity, lacking only the raw material of creation itself. This he took from [[Yggdrasil]], exchanging [[The Eidolon of Prognos|his right eye]] for the privilege. [[The Eidolon of Prognos|His eye]] abandoned in [[The Well of Mimir|the well at the base of Yggdrasil]], [[The AllFather]] triumphantly commenced the creation of his world, and established [[The Church of the Allfather|his church]]. As the world's logos, [[The AllFather]] provided stability, order, and in that order his creation thrived. Over time, as [[Yggdrasil]] grew hungry to reclaim the material that belonged to it, it sent [[The Heralds|its heralds]] into the world, and [[The AllFather]]'s power waned as the people began to believe more in his church than in him. Eventually, with [[the execution of the last true believer as a heretic]], [[The AllFather]] died, and [[Yggdrasil]] attempted to reclaim the reality that had so long resisted it. But [[Yggdrasil]]'s efforts were stymied, as, in the act of his dying, [[The AllFather]] had shattered, spreading [[The Eidolons]], fragments of the logos throughout the world, and they acted to stabilize it, resisting the reclamation of [[Yggdrasil]]. Now the world hovers in a liminal-yet-decaying state, parts of it reclaimed by [[Yggdrasil]]'s great roots, while yet others continue to resist.
