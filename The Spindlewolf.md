A savage and titanic white wolf, bearing no face, but instead, a writhing mass of protruding light blue tentacles, translucent and slightly aglow. The Spindlewolf hunts liars, or, rather, it hunts their lies. Those who are its prey have their illusions taken from them and even seem unable to lie, for a time. There are those who seek the Spindlewolf intentionally, even those who have wandered into the wild, lying, in the hopes of luring it into a trap. But so far it has evaded any attempts to capture it.
